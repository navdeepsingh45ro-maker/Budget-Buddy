Help & FAQ

Getting started

How do I add an expense?

Tap the + button. Type the amount and a description, or tap the 🎤 mic and say it ("Spent 450 on Swiggy with UPI"), or tap the 📷 camera to scan a receipt. Check the details, then tap Log Expense.

How do I set my budget?

Go to Budget → Update Budget. With "Carry budget into each new month" on (Settings → Budget), next month starts with the same amount automatically.

Can I edit or delete an expense?

Yes. Go to History, then tap the ✏️ pencil to edit or the 🗑️ bin to delete.

Buddy and insights

What is the Coach Insight?

It's a short message on your dashboard about what matters most right now: your pace, how much you can spend per day, or a category that's running high. The numbers are calculated by the app from your own data.

Where do the coaching tips come from?

After each month ends, Buddy writes your monthly report with a few ideas to help you save. During the next month, the Coach shows one of those ideas each day.

What can I ask Buddy?

Questions about your own spending: "How much can I spend per day?", "Where did most of my money go last month?". Buddy answers from your data and doesn't give investment or tax advice.

How do I see a monthly report?

Go to Budget → Monthly Report and pick any month that has expenses. Last month's report is shown by default.

Notifications

What notifications will I get?

Bills due tomorrow, an evening nudge if you haven't logged anything that day, a weekly summary on Monday mornings, your monthly report on the 1st, and budget alerts. Choose which ones in Settings → Notifications.

Will you notify me at night?

The app's own messages are never sent between 10 PM and 8 AM. Alerts about things you set up, like a recurring bill, can still arrive.

I'm not getting notifications.

Check that push is on in Settings → Notifications for this device, that your browser or phone allows notifications for Budget Buddy, and that Do Not Disturb or Focus is off. Use Send test notification to check.

How do I get notifications on iPhone?

Open Budget Buddy in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen, open it from your Home Screen, then turn notifications on in Settings. This needs iOS 16.4 or later.

Account and data

I forgot my password.

On the login screen tap Forgot Password?. We'll email you a 6-digit code.

How do I change my name or password?

Go to Settings → Account.

How do I export my data?

Go to Settings → Your data → Export, as CSV, Excel or PDF.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Your data → Delete account. This permanently deletes everything and can't be undone.

Is my data safe? Do you sell it?

We never sell your data or show ads. See the Privacy Policy for details.
