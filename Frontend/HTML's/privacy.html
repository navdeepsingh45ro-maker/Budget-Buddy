Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Budget Buddy ("we", "the app") helps you track expenses and budgets. This policy explains what we collect, why, who it's shared with, and the choices you have. We wrote it in plain language. If anything is unclear, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password. Your password is stored only as a secure one-way hash; we can't read it.
  • What you enter: expenses (amount, category, merchant, note, payment method, date), budgets, recurring expenses, and your settings.
  • Receipt photos: when you scan a receipt, the photo is sent to our AI provider to read it. We don't store the photo.
  • Voice input: speech-to-text is done by your browser (Apple, Google or Microsoft, depending on your device). We only receive the resulting text.
  • Notifications: if you turn on push notifications, we store a technical address for your device that lets us send them. No location or contacts.
  • Sign-up choices: your age group (13–17 or 18+), when you accepted the Terms and this policy, and, if you're 13–17, that a parent or guardian agreed.
  • Technical basics: your device type (from your browser) and the times you sign in, to keep the service working and secure.

We do not collect your bank login, card numbers, UPI PIN, contacts, location or SMS messages.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show your dashboard, history, budgets and reports.
  • Coaching and reminders: we calculate your insights from your own data; reminders and summaries are sent based on your notification settings.
  • Account emails: password-reset codes. We don't send marketing emails.

AI features (Google Gemini)

Some features use Google's Gemini AI: reading receipt photos, understanding voice entries, "Ask Buddy" chat, coaching tips and the commentary in monthly reports.

  • For chat, tips and reports we send calculated totals (for example, "Food: ₹3,550 this month") and a short list of recent expenses with date, amount, category and merchant. We do not send your notes, name or email.
  • For receipt scanning we send the photo; for voice entries, the text you spoke.
  • [IMPORTANT, CHOOSE ONE BEFORE LAUNCH] (a) Paid Gemini API: Google processes this data to provide the service and does not use it to train its models. (b) Free Gemini API: Google may use this data to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. Don't include sensitive personal information in receipts, notes or chat.
  • All numbers you see (totals, limits, days left) are calculated by the app itself, not by the AI.

Who we share data with

We never sell your data and we don't show ads. We share data only with services that run the app for us:

  • Hosting and database: [HOSTING PROVIDER], which stores the app's data.
  • Google (Gemini API): AI features, as described above.
  • Google (Gmail): sends password-reset emails.
  • Push services (Apple, Google, Mozilla): deliver notifications to your device. Notification content is encrypted end-to-end to your device.

We may disclose data if required by law.

Cookies and tracking

We use no cookies, analytics, ads or tracking tools, and we don't record what you do on screen. Fonts and code are served from our own app, not from third parties. Your browser keeps your sign-in token and a few display preferences on your device; logging out removes the sign-in token.

Keeping your data safe

Passwords are hashed, connections use HTTPS, and each account can only access its own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your account and all of its data are deleted immediately from our live database. Backups, if any, are overwritten within [30] days.

Your choices and rights

  • See and export everything: Settings → Your data → Export (CSV, Excel or PDF).
  • Correct your name, expenses and budgets at any time in the app.
  • Delete your account and all data: Settings → Your data → Delete account.
  • Notifications: turn any type on or off in Settings, or turn push off for a device.

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or the GDPR), you may have further rights. Contact us to use them.

Children and young people

Budget Buddy is for people aged 13 and over, including students. If you're 13–17, you need a parent or guardian to agree before you sign up. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13: sign-up is refused and nothing from the attempt is stored. If we learn that a child under 13 has an account, we delete it. We never track, profile or show ads to anyone, including users under 18. A parent or guardian can contact us to see or delete their child's data.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll tell you in the app before they take effect.
